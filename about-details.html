Deep Dive

Our Story & Musical Journey

How eight individual musicians came together in Bandarawela to reshape the hill country live music scene.

The Origin

It began in early pandemic days as acoustic porch sessions in Bandarawela. Surrounded by misty hills, we brought together empty instrument cases, raw intuition, and a shared hunger for organic rhythm. No digital tracks, no synthetic covers—just voice, wood, and steel strings.

Why the Eight-Piece Setup works

Managing eight active musical viewpoints isn’t easy, but it’s our superpower. Having independent keys, two acoustic guitar lines, comprehensive live percussion, separate dynamic drum layers, and a hip-hop/rap vocal integration gives us the versatility to perform classical covers, heavy retro medleys, and modern pop pulses back-to-back seamlessly.

"We don’t just play songs; we redesign them into raw acoustic soundscapes that fill massive dynamic arenas yet feel completely personal."
