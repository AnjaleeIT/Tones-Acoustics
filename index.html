Who we are

The band

TONES ACOUSTIC is an eight-member acoustic collective based in Bandarawela, proudly known as one of the go-to live bands in Badulla for functions of every kind — weddings, corporate events, festivals, and private parties.

What started as a small hill country jam session has grown into a full eight piece live act, blending acoustic guitars, percussion, keys, and layered vocals into a sound that feels just as at home in an intimate hall as it does on an open-air festival stage.
